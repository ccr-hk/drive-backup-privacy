PROJECTMAP BACKUP / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

What the app accesses

The app uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to create, read, and update backup files and folders it creates or opens in the operator’s Drive. It cannot browse unrelated Drive files through this permission.

How data is used

Backup files are copied from the operator’s computer to the operator’s Google Drive. The app reads uploaded files to check transfers. It does not sell backup data or share it with other users. This website contains no backup files and no analytics scripts.

Storage and deletion

Backup files remain on the operator’s computer and in Google Drive until the operator deletes them. Deleting a local copy does not automatically delete its Drive copy. OAuth credentials are stored on the operator’s computer, not on this website. The operator can revoke access in their Google Account settings and delete the Drive folder.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the operator’s GitHub profile.
